Section~\ref{sec:benchmark} states the ground-truth finding. This is the detail behind it.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=0.15em]
  \item \textbf{How it surfaced.} Tightening the two eICU bounds in the shared rule file
        (Section~\ref{sec:fair}) made the four eICU cells score \emph{worse}. The corrected bound
        flags the 99.8 degrees Celsius body temperature, and the scoring calls that a false
        positive.
  \item \textbf{The rest of the allowlist.} \texttt{known\_corrupt\_values} names the eICU records a
        truthful bound is right to flag. Besides the temperature reading these are three FiO2
        readings of 4000 and 10000 that no scored cell reaches, which is why the counterfactual of
        Section~\ref{sec:benchmark} moves on the temperature row alone.
  \item \textbf{The four false negatives}, where the analyte's legitimate ceiling sits above the
        9999 the legacy injector writes. These are the eICU enzyme-activity aggregate (code 1,
        Units/L), MIMIC 50878 for aspartate aminotransferase, and MIMIC 51108 and 51109 for urine
        volume.
\end{itemize}

Section~\ref{sec:exposure} points here for the Encounter/Observation split. Of the 134 event
resources in each cohort, Synthea contributes 23 Encounters and 111 Observations, eICU-CRD 7 and
127.
