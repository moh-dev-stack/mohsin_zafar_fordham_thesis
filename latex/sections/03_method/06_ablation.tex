The controlled comparison gives both systems the same fields, so it cannot price the boundary. The
ablation narrows the manifest past what the check needs and rescores. That is the study's primary
measurement. One \textbf{arm} withholds a single JSONPath from the Confined manifests, everything
else held fixed, and five arms run over the same twelve cells at rate 0.30. Three withhold a field
the plausibility check \textbf{consumes}, the analyte code, the value and the unit.
\texttt{control\_minimal} withholds nothing, fixing what an unmodified manifest scores;
\texttt{ucum\_code} withholds \texttt{\$.valueQuantity.code}, released by each width and read by no
check, the negative control.

An arm's cost is the largest absolute gap between Baseline and Confined across its twelve cells, in
percentage points of F1, reported alongside the mean Confined F1 over those cells.
Section~\ref{sec:ablationhard} re-runs all five under the hard injector.
