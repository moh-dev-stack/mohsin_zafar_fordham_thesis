\subsection{Cohorts}
\label{sec:data}

Three public FHIR-shaped cohorts supply all the results.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=0.15em]
  \item \textbf{Synthea} (Walonoski et al., 2018).
  \item \textbf{MIMIC-IV Clinical Database Demo v2.2} (Johnson, Bulgarelli, Pollard, et al., 2023;
        full release in Johnson, Bulgarelli, Shen, et al., 2023). All 100 subjects are converted.
  \item \textbf{eICU Collaborative Research Database Demo v2.0.1} (Johnson et al., 2021; full release
        in Pollard et al., 2018). From its 2{,}500-plus stays the study takes a
        \textbf{100-stay subsample}, one Encounter per Patient.
\end{itemize}

A \textbf{cell} is one scored run at one seed, cohort and manifest-width setting. A seed-by-cohort
table has twelve cells, a seed-pooled cohort-by-width table nine, and a table crossing all three has
36. Every cell evaluates $N = 200$ resources, 66 of them Patient records.

The artefact of record is the converted bundles, not the source CSVs, and the adapter ships, so both
PhysioNet cohorts rebuild. Synthea cannot. No generator seed was recorded, leaving it verifiable
only by digest.
